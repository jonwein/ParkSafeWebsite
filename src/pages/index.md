# ParkSafe

### Never Get a Parking Ticket

ParkSafe is a parking assistance app for New York City. View real-time parking sign data on a map, track where you parked, and get reminders before restrictions start — so you never have to worry about getting a ticket.

## Features

### Real-Time Parking Sign Map

See every parking sign near you on an interactive map. Signs are color-coded so you can tell at a glance what's safe, what's restricted, and what's expiring soon.

### Park Your Car

Save your parked location and see exactly when you need to move. ParkSafe shows a live countdown based on the parking signs at your spot.

### Smart Reminders

Get notified before a restriction starts so you can move your car in time. Customize how far in advance you want to be reminded.

### Automatic Parking Detection

Connect ParkSafe to your CarPlay or Bluetooth and it will automatically detect when you park — no need to open the app.

### Alternate Side Parking Alerts

Stay on top of ASP schedules and get notified when alternate side parking is suspended for holidays.

### Home Screen Widget

See your parked location, countdown timer, and ASP status right from your home screen without opening the app.

## Privacy First

ParkSafe is designed with your privacy in mind. We only use your location in real-time to show nearby parking signs — we don't store it, track it, or share it. Your reminders stay on your device. ParkSafe doesn't ask for your name, email, or an account, and never shares your GPS location with advertisers. The app is supported by ads, which you can remove with an in-app purchase.
